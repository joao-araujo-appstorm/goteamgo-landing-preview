Questions before your first workout that counts.

Didn't find yours? Write to contato@goteamgo.com.br.

How much does it cost?

Creating an account and using the app is free. Some challenges charge an entry fee, and when they accept both modes you can join for free without competing for the prize.

How does the cash prize work?

Those who pay the entry fee compete for the paid podium. The prize is the sum of the entry fees, split among the Top 3, and you are charged on your first post, by Pix or card.

Can I train with friends?

Yes. Create a group, send the code or the link and follow the group ranking together. Or challenge someone to an X1.

How do levels work?

Every point counts toward your path: 60 levels in 6 leagues, from Beginner to Pro Marathoner.

Do I need a watch or Strava?

No. A photo of your workout is enough. Strava and Google Health are optional.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. There's an app on the App Store and Google Play, and it also opens in the browser.

Questions before running your first challenge.

Didn't find yours? Write to contato@goteamgo.com.br or talk to the team.

Talk to the team
What is GoTeamGo?

A platform to create gamified experiences with challenges, missions, points, rankings, teams, achievements and results tracking.

Can the challenges be customized?

Yes. Missions, rules, campaign branding, points, goals and prizes are set up to match the project's goal.

Can I use it for short or ongoing campaigns?

Yes. It works for one-off actions, events or recurring engagement programs.

Can I track the results?

Yes. The dashboard brings together participation, activities and campaign metrics, including CO2 savings and an ESG report in PDF.

Do people need to install anything?

No. GoTeamGo opens in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen. There's also an app on the App Store and Google Play.

How do you prevent fake activity?

Each challenge picks the proof: an on-site photo, a photo taken on the spot, Strava or Google Health steps. Posts go through manual, automatic or AI approval, and anyone can report a post.

What about employee data?

Each company has a separate database and can limit sign-up to company emails. Google Health only reads steps, and names are shortened in prize draws.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the campaign format. Request a demo and get a proposal.
